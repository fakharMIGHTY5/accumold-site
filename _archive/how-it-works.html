The pipeline

What happens in the six seconds after you tap the shutter.

Nothing here is magic, and nothing here is a stock image classifier. Each stage narrows the answer until the app can say something useful in plain English.

Important

An AccuMold report is a risk assessment, not a laboratory result. It tells you how likely a problem is and how urgently to act. Confirming species or spore counts is a job for a certified inspector — which is what the consultation is for.

01

Five quick questions

Where in the home, how long it has been there, whether there is a known leak, whether anyone is getting symptoms, and what the surface is. Context the photo can't give.

02

Surface capture

One photo. The app checks framing and light before it accepts the shot, because a blurred, backlit picture produces a worthless answer.

03

Texture and discolouration mapping

The image is broken into regions and each is scored on tone, saturation and edge structure. Growth reads differently from dirt, shadow and old paint — it spreads with soft, irregular boundaries rather than straight ones.

04

Conditions cross-reference

Local temperature and relative humidity are pulled for your location and weighted against the visual score. The same stain means something very different at 45% humidity than it does at 68%.

05

Risk classification

Low, moderate or high — using the same three-level language and the same colour coding as the app's dashboard, so it always means the same thing.

06

Expert analysis, written out

A short written explanation of why it landed where it did, and what to do next. No jargon you have to look up.
