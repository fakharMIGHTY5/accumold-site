Start here

Three habits that prevent most of it.

Clean regularly

Faucets, drains and damp surfaces hold buildup that gives spores something to feed on. Removing the residue removes the food.

Dry surfaces

Moisture is mold's best friend. A squeegee on the shower wall and a window open for ten minutes after a bath does more than any spray.

Watch the humidity

Above 60% and the clock starts. A cheap hygrometer, or the app's dashboard, tells you when a room has crossed the line.

Room by room

Where it actually starts.

These are the six zones the app inspects, and what to check in each before you scan.

Grout lines, the silicone seal around the bath, the ceiling directly above the shower, and the back of the extractor grille. If the extractor doesn't hold a sheet of paper against it when running, it isn't working.

Under the sink — the single most common starting point in the house. Also the wall behind the washing machine, the seal on the fridge door, and the back corners of base units on an exterior wall.

North-facing exterior walls, behind furniture pushed flat against them, and the reveals around window frames. Cold surfaces plus still air is where condensation settles and stays.

Trickle vents painted shut, extractor ducting that vents into a loft rather than outside, and blocked airbricks. Most damp problems in otherwise sound homes are ventilation problems.

Bubbling, flaking or discoloured paint is moisture telling you where it is. Painting over it traps the moisture and hides the evidence — scan it before you cover it.

Bleach removes the colour, not the colony — on a porous surface the roots survive and it returns. If a patch comes back after cleaning, that's your signal to scan rather than scrub again.

Know the line

When to stop cleaning and start scanning.

It comes back within a fortnight of cleaning it.

It covers more than about a square metre.

There's a musty smell you can't locate.

Someone in the house has developed a cough, a rash, or worsening asthma.

Any one of those, scan it. It's free, it takes a minute, and a dated record from today is worth a great deal in three months' time.
