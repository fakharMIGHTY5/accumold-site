The problem

You can see something.
You just can't prove it.

01 — UNCERTAINTY

No idea if it's serious

A stain on the ceiling. A smell in the hallway. Nothing tells you whether it's cosmetic or structural — so you do nothing.

02 — POWERLESSNESS

Renters get ignored

Reporting it isn't enough. Without documentation, a landlord can call it condensation and move on. You need evidence with a timestamp.

03 — FRICTION

Inspections are slow and expensive

Booking a professional means waiting days and paying hundreds — before anyone can tell you whether there was a problem at all.

Spot · Scan · Report · Act

STEP 01

Spot it.

Something looks wrong. A dark patch behind the toilet, a bloom on the bedroom ceiling, a warped skirting board. Open the app.

STEP 02

Scan it.

Five quick questions and one photo. The app reads the surface, cross-references the humidity and temperature where you are, and starts working.

STEP 03

Report it.

An instant risk level in plain English, with the reasoning written out. No jargon, no diagnosis you need a dictionary for.

STEP 04

Act on it.

Keep the report. Send it to your landlord. Or book a live consultation with a certified inspector, right there in the app.
