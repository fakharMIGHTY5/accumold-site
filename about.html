How they find it

Thermal first. Open the wall last.

Moisture Master Pros were among the first in the region to put FLIR thermal cameras into a diagnostic kit. Wet material holds a different temperature to dry material, so a leak shows up as a cold shape through the paint — before anyone cuts anything.

Drones cover the rest: roofs, upper stucco and anywhere a ladder is the slow answer. It is the same instinct behind the app — look first, and only open what you have to.
