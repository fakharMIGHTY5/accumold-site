The homeowner · 35–60

Know for sure,
before it becomes
a bigger problem.

After the storm. After the leak under the sink. Or just because the box room smells different this month and there's a child sleeping in it. You don't want a diagnosis — you want to stop wondering.

  • Check the rooms your family actually sleeps in, one at a time
  • Add family health conditions so the advice accounts for asthma and allergies
  • Continuous humidity and temperature monitoring between scans
“Your home is where you should feel safest.”

The stakeholder · 30–65

Screen a unit
before you sign for it.

You're not worried — you're calculating. A remediation you didn't price into the offer is the difference between a good deal and a bad one, and you're looking at more than one property this month.

  • Run a whole-house inspection on a viewing, in the time it takes to walk the rooms
  • A dated record per unit, so a portfolio has a paper trail rather than a memory
  • Escalate straight to a certified inspector when a unit warrants a closer look
