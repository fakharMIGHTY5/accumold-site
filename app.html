What a report says

Three numbers, one colour, no jargon.

  • Low Conditions are not favourable. Keep an eye on it.
  • Moderate You're near the threshold. Dry it out, re-scan in 48 hours.
  • High Conditions actively favour growth. Get it looked at.

Every report also explains why it landed there, in a short written analysis you can download, keep and send on.
