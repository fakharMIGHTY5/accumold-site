The report

Written like a person explaining it, not a system logging it.

Every report gives you the risk level, the conditions it was based on, and a paragraph of expert analysis explaining the reasoning. That paragraph is the difference between knowing something is wrong and being able to say why.

Sample — expert analysis

“The current conditions of 93°F temperature and 55% humidity indicate a very low risk level for mold growth and indoor air quality issues. At this temperature and humidity, the environment is not conducive to mold proliferation. However, it is important to maintain vigilance as changes in conditions could alter the risk level.”
